1. **Permanent execution guard is not a production implementation** (51-54; 480-487). Every execution always throws, including explicit require_matched_replay:false. Latest requested policy supersedes earlier unconditional replay-bypass prohibition: a future explicitly requested paper run with replay optional may proceed UNVERIFIED once other readiness/security requirements are implemented. Required replay must still block without trusted evidence; validation_only must never dispatch/write. Implement truthful required/optional replay admission and verification labels, without treating synthetic checks or caller claims as proof. Keep original executable match A2A/settlement/evidence and batch between-match review paths; do not replace them with this permanently blocked entry or validation-only aggregators.

2. **Journal publication is not an atomic single-writer commit** (20-33, 63-76, 216-219). Journal-before-pointer correctly supplies a recovery source for a missing pointer, so pointer delete-before-write alone is no longer the earlier lost-ledger defect. However put is check-then-write, not exclusive creation/CAS; there is no per-run lock/reservation. Two settlers can both observe a missing round journal and publish different versions, both passing readback at different moments. A torn journal is fatal to stateText even when the older pointer is intact. Require enforced serialization plus atomic immutable journal publication/integrity and a defined torn-write recovery protocol before concurrent or restart-safe execution is claimed.

3. **Committed recovery unnecessarily depends on current market availability and new-run admission** (181-194, 221-224, 498). settle/finalize call loadMarket and executionGuard BEFORE examining a cached private settlement. Removing/changing the source CSV after a successful private commit prevents republishing missing results/evidence or obtaining final balances, although the journal contains market_text and the recorded accounting. Separate cached recovery from new admission: validate authority/config and use the pinned private snapshot for recovery, never reapply orders. Source drift must block new decisions rather than erase access to committed evidence.

4. **Path isolation is not enforced authorization/reservation** (41-45, 79, 101, 206, 473-477). Batch/track/match/agent paths are distinct, but no scope adapter or reservation protects judge/state/journal (including future bars), private submissions or each agent's strategy/account files. Prevent competitors from reading future judge data or writing judge/peer ledgers; reserve namespaces exclusively, and distinguish legitimate recovery from unrelated reuse. Unlike the previous pure kernel, this candidate does NOT trust caller engine_state: it re-normalizes inputs, loads filesystem state and binds settings. The remaining forged-state risk is unauthorized filesystem access, not the previously demonstrated routine-state equity injection. Do not reintroduce routine-state authority when splicing the graph. Effective per-track history/bars are now mapped in loadMarket; preserve that mapping in actual native dispatch.

5. **Read-valid inputs can exceed the persistence budget** (26, 57, 101-103, 216). The same exclusive 2MiB bound applies to CSV and to the serialized entire state/journal containing that CSV plus settings, escaped newlines and cumulative rounds/trades/manifests. A valid CSV of 2MiB-minus-one bytes necessarily produces an oversized state, so preflight can pass but preparation cannot persist; later rounds can similarly exceed capacity. Preflight must account for durable representation/growth, or use separately bounded immutable snapshots and compact journal records. Do not relax completeness bounds or silently truncate accounting.

6. **No immutable accepted-submission/provenance intake** (144-155, 206-216). Legacy CSV/manifest parsing, file-order single-next-close execution and per-agent all-or-nothing rejection are present; they are not the old pure-kernel protocol blockers. But orders and manifest are separately reread from mutable paths, with no frozen intake/version or exact raw payloads/code hashes retained for independent reconstruction. A producer can change either file during intake or after a crash before the first commit; failed/malformed payloads are especially unreconstructable from summary/failure strings. Bind immutable raw orders+manifest, actual source/code references, normalized config and pinned market snapshot to each accepted/rejected round before settlement; reuse them on resume and for independent replay. Existing post-commit cached results must remain authoritative and must never reread/reapply changed orders.
